A plain-language read on whether a stock is cheap, fair, or expensive versus its own sector — P/E, P/B, ROE, leverage, dividend yield, and now 5-year revenue vs opex growth and a price trend. Prototype covering four PSX benchmark names.

New to these terms? Quick definitions
P/E ratio
Price ÷ earnings per share. Lower usually means cheaper, relative to peers.
P/B ratio
Price ÷ book value per share. What you pay versus the company's net assets.
ROE
Return on equity. How efficiently the company turns shareholder capital into profit.
D/E
Debt ÷ equity. Higher means more leverage, and more risk if rates or earnings turn.
Revenue vs opex growth
If costs grow faster than sales, margins shrink even while sales look healthy.
CAGR
Compound annual growth rate — the smoothed yearly growth rate over several years.
